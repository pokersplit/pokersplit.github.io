Playing a game

How do I start a game?

Tap ＋ on the home screen, set the buy-in and chips, and add your players.

How do I record a buy-in or rebuy?

Open the game and tap a player. From there you can buy in, return chips, borrow, lend or repay.

How do loans between players work?

Record a loan when one player gives chips to another, and record every repayment so it isn't counted twice. Unpaid loans are included when you settle, so nobody pays twice.

Can someone join late?

Yes. Open the game and tap the person + button.

Settling up

How do I cash out?

Count each player's chips and enter them, or enter 0 for anyone who busted. When the chips balance, tap Settle Up.

The chips don't balance. What now?

The chips counted don't match the chips the bank handed out. Recount the stacks, then check for a missing buy-in or return.

What does Result mean?

Result = Ended with − Put in. Plus means you won, and minus means you lost. Put in is what you bought from the bank, plus chips borrowed, minus chips lent or returned.

Does PokerSplit move money?

No. It only does the math. Your group pays each other however you like, and you can tick payments off in the checklist.

iCloud and your data

How do I move my games to a new phone?

With Sync with iCloud on, sign in with the same Apple Account on the new phone. You can also use Settings → Restore from iCloud.

Sync isn't working.

Check that you're signed in to iCloud and online, then try Settings → Sync Now in PokerSplit.

How do I delete my games?

Delete one from the home screen, or use Settings → Delete All Games. With sync on, this also removes them from iCloud and your other devices. It can't be undone.

How do I hide store promotions?

Turn off Show Promotions in Settings, under Our Other Stores.
