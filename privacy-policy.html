PokerSplit · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

PokerSplit: Home Game ("the App") is published by Berry Shops LLC ("we," "us"). It keeps score of home poker games. We do not collect your information through the App.

The short version

  • No account, sign-in, analytics, third-party ads or tracking.
  • Your games stay on your device and, if sync is on, in your own private iCloud.
  • You can delete your games at any time.

Information you enter

Game names, player names, buy-ins, chip counts, loans, results and settings are stored on your device. Player names can be nicknames.

iCloud

iCloud sync is on by default when your device is signed in to iCloud. It stores your games in your private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), which we cannot read. You can turn it off in the App's Settings. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Sharing

If you share a game's results, you choose what app and people to send them to.

Our other stores

The App may show a card for our other stores, Berry Shops and se.lo. When to show it is decided on your device. Tapping it opens the App Store or glowithselo.com inside the App; those services have their own privacy practices and may collect information. Turn the cards off with Show Promotions in Settings.

Gambling and payments

The App does not offer gambling, process payments or handle money.

Deleting your data

Delete games in the App at any time. With sync on, they are also removed from iCloud. Deleting the App removes its data from that device but leaves games in iCloud, so use Delete All Games first if you want those removed too.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your address and message and use them to reply.

Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the date at the top.
